# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026

We stand behind the SME Invoice & Payment Tracker Kit. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, we offer refunds under the terms outlined below.

## Refund eligibility

You may request a refund if:
- You purchased the kit fewer than **14 days ago**
- The kit does not work as described
- You cannot access or download your purchase
- You changed your mind (no questions asked)

You are **not** eligible for a refund if:
- More than 14 days have passed since purchase
- You purchased the kit and did not attempt to use it before requesting a refund
- You have shared the kit with others or distributed it

## How to request a refund

1. Email hello@lemonlogicai.com with:
   - Your receipt number or email address used to purchase
   - Brief reason for the refund request
   - Date of purchase

2. We will review your request within 48 hours

3. If approved, we will issue a refund to your original payment method (usually within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank)

## Refund process timing

- **Approval:** 48 hours
- **Payment processing:** 3–5 business days (bank dependent)
- **Total:** Up to 7 business days

Note: Your bank may take additional time to process the refund. If you don't see the refund after 7 days, contact your bank or email us.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds are not offered. Refunds are for the full purchase price only, minus any payment processing fees charged by the payment processor.

## Special circumstances

### Technical issues
If you purchased the kit and cannot access or download it due to a technical error, we will:
1. Attempt to troubleshoot and resolve the issue
2. Reissue the kit in an alternative format if needed
3. Provide a full refund if the issue cannot be resolved

### Questions before purchasing
If you have questions about whether the kit is right for you before purchasing, email hello@lemonlogicai.com. We can help you decide.

## Product improvements

Occasionally, we may release updates or improvements to the kit. These are provided free to existing customers (when applicable). Updates do not affect refund eligibility.

## Disputes with payment processor

If your refund request is related to a payment dispute (e.g., unauthorized charge, duplicate charge), the payment processor (Selar) may investigate. In such cases:
- Contact Selar directly for dispute resolution
- Provide us with any communication from Selar
- We will cooperate fully with investigations
