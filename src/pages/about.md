## The person behind this

I'm Eric Olafisoye, founder of LemonLogic Lab. I work with Nigerian businesses — including LA AutoWorks (a mechanic firm in Lagos operating for over 20 years), OK3D (logistics), Andstan Solar (clean energy), and Unique Ojali (fashion design) — helping them move from manual processes to proper automation and tools.

I didn't start LemonLogic Lab because I wanted to be a blogger or a "thought leader." I started it because I was tired of solving the same problems over and over in my own businesses, and I kept seeing other Nigerian SMEs struggle with the exact same things.

## Why this exists

When you run an SME in Nigeria — whether it's auto repair, logistics, or any service business — you're not just doing the work. You're also invoicing, chasing payments, calculating VAT, managing clients, and trying not to lose money to bad record-keeping.

Most of the advice on the internet about business assumes you're in the US or UK. Invoicing guides don't mention Nigerian tax rates. Payment advice doesn't account for the fact that most of your clients pay via bank transfer, not card. Accounting software is overkill and costs money you don't have.

So I built the SME Invoice & Payment Tracker Kit to solve the problem I kept running into helping these businesses automate their invoicing. And I started writing about it — not to sell you something, but because I've actually lived these problems and figured out what works.

## What you'll read here

Every article on this site is based on real experience running businesses in Nigeria. The advice on calculating VAT? That's from doing it. The guide on getting clients to pay on time? That's from chasing payments. The article on invoicing mistakes? That's from making them.

This isn't theoretical. It's practical.

That said, nothing on this site is professional tax, accounting, or legal advice. If you need that, talk to an accountant or lawyer in your jurisdiction. What's here is "here's what worked for me and other business owners."

## The invoice kit

The kit is a spreadsheet — Excel or Google Sheets — that automates what took me 30 minutes every week to do manually. It calculates VAT at 7.5%, tracks which clients paid and which haven't, and produces invoices you can send immediately.

I use it with the businesses I work with. Other Nigerian SMEs use it too. If it works for you, great. If it doesn't, there's a refund.
