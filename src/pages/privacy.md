# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026

LemonLogic Lab ("we," "us," or "our") operates the lemonlogiclab.com website and sells the SME Invoice & Payment Tracker Kit. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## Information we collect

### When you visit the website
- Your IP address
- Browser type and operating system
- Pages visited and time spent
- Referrer source
- Basic analytics through our hosting provider

### When you purchase the invoice kit
Through our payment processor (Selar):
- Your name
- Your email address
- Payment information (processed by Selar, not stored by us)
- Transaction details

### When you contact us
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your WhatsApp number (if you provide it)
- The content of your message

We do **not** collect sensitive personal data (health, financial details beyond payment, government IDs, etc.) unless you voluntarily provide it.

## How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:
- Process and deliver your invoice kit purchase
- Send you confirmation emails and delivery instructions
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Understand how the website is used and improve it
- Comply with legal obligations

## Cookies

Our website may use cookies to:
- Remember your preferences
- Track basic analytics (if enabled)
- Improve user experience

Most browsers allow you to reject cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may limit some site functionality.

## Third-party services

### Selar (payment processor)
When you purchase the invoice kit, payment is processed by Selar. Your payment information is handled according to Selar's privacy policy, not ours. We do not store your payment details.

### Hosting provider (Cloudflare/Netlify)
Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Workers and Netlify, which may collect basic traffic and security data. Refer to their privacy policies for details.

### Email service (if applicable)
If you subscribe to any newsletter or updates, we use email services that are compliant with data protection standards.

## Data retention

- Purchase records: Retained for tax and business purposes (typically 3–5 years)
- Support emails: Retained until the issue is resolved, then deleted
- Analytics data: Automatically deleted after 90 days

You have the right to:
- Request what personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data

- Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, email hello@lemonlogicai.com with your request.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no online transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
